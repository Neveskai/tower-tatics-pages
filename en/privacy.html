Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

Summary

Piggy against the World does not collect, store, process or share users’ personal or identifying data. The game does not require an internet connection to work and does not request any permissions to access device data (location, camera, contacts, storage, etc.).

Data collected

None. The app has no account system, login, telemetry, analytics or advertising. All player progress is saved locally on the device.

Data sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If future features involve data collection (e.g. cloud synchronization or advertising), this policy will be updated in advance and the new version will be published on this same page.
